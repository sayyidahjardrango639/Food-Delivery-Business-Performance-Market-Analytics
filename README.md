# 🍔 Food Delivery Business Performance & Market Analytics

## 📖 Project Overview

The **Food Delivery Business Performance & Market Analytics** project focuses on analysing food delivery orders, revenue performance, customer satisfaction, delivery efficiency, city wise performance, and restaurant category trends using Business Analytics techniques. The project combines **Microsoft Excel, Python, MySQL Workbench, and Microsoft Power BI** to transform raw food delivery data into meaningful business insights and interactive visualisations. The dashboard supports data driven decision making by providing clear insights into revenue generation, customer behaviour, city performance, restaurant category performance, and delivery efficiency.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyse food delivery order and customer data.
- Evaluate total revenue and average order value.
- Identify high performing and low performing cities.
- Analyse restaurant category wise revenue performance.
- Monitor customer satisfaction through customer ratings.
- Analyse delivery time performance.
- Identify monthly revenue trends.
- Identify high value customers based on order value.
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard for business decision making.

## 🔍 Key Findings

- The total revenue generated during the analysed period was **₹117,420**.
- The average customer rating was **4.04**, indicating generally positive customer satisfaction.
- **Kochi** was the best performing city, generating the highest revenue of **₹24,130**.
- **Biryani** was the strongest performing restaurant category, generating **₹40,200** in revenue.
- **Café** was the weakest performing restaurant category, generating **₹7,620** in revenue.
- **April** recorded the highest monthly revenue of **₹32,040**.
- The analysis indicated an opportunity to improve customer satisfaction through faster and more efficient delivery.
- The analysis also identified opportunities to strengthen underperforming restaurant categories.

---

## 💡 Recommendations

- Improve overall performance in the **Kollam market** by expanding restaurant availability and promoting underperforming food categories.
- Improve customer satisfaction in **Calicut** by reducing delivery times and strengthening lower performing restaurant categories.
- Introduce seasonal and trending food promotions to improve revenue during weaker months.
- Strengthen **Desserts and Café** categories through new food varieties, discounts, combo offers, and targeted promotions while maintaining the strong performance of Biryani.
- Reduce long delivery times through better route planning, improved restaurant coordination, and efficient delivery operations.
